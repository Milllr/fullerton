# Fullerton Productions site

Portfolio for James Downey (Miller's dad), Lighting Designer, Fullerton Productions, Toronto. Vite + vanilla TypeScript, no framework.

- The site is the `tungsten` rebuild, merged to main on 2026-10-23 (PR #2; production https://fullerton-b9y.pages.dev): a full rebuild under a new aesthetic Miller asked for on 2026-10-22, from his mood board (Nissan Le Mans 1998 ad, Dstrukt "Fraktur.Ation", grainy cream and orange gradient blobs, a half frosted architecture card, a Masako Ohta record sleeve, a Jeep topo map ad, and the Unveil studio site's stacked project planes). Highly minimal, warm 2000s gradient, tech type, artistic interaction applied sparingly. The old dark stage site (Vectorworks drawings, grandMA3 desk) is in history up to 08df3b7; its rules no longer apply.
- Miller's notes on the first pass (2026-10-22): the hanging bulb was tacky and too forced, so it's gone. Keep the warmth subtle: a wide, soft trail of warmth under the page wherever the cursor goes, and an equally satisfying touch interaction on phones. See README, The warmth.
- Work and credits are one Work section (Miller): a pinned showcase you scroll and hover through, a clear way into each show, then all his work in one list. Miller's taste (2026-10-23): no boxed buttons (View the show is a link on a hairline), no photo counts or extra link columns, previews pop up above the pointer, every name that points at a show is a link to it. See README, The work.
- The cover's element of interest is a window gobo (light through a window across the paper, leaves drifting, the pointer's shadow in it); see README, The warmth.
- The scrollbar is a DMX cable (Miller's idea): male XLR at the scroll position, slack coiling over/under at the bottom, thin, realistic and quiet. The over/under must read (Miller, 2026-10-23): alternate loops turned, crossings over then under the bundle. src/cable/cable.ts.
- His logo (bulb in sunglasses, script wordmark) is traced to SVG in src/brand/ and used in the header, at Contact and as the favicon.
- Things that come up do so on a modelled tungsten filament (src/light/tungsten.ts): dim and amber, then clear; cooling leaves an amber afterglow. The warmth cools the same way.
- Type: Michroma for the few display words, Fragment Mono for everything else. Ink on paper; colour comes from the light. No dash characters as separators (they read as dashes in copy); use hairline rules.
- Motion rule from James's lighting doctrine: nothing snaps. Everything moves on damped springs or the filament's own curve.
- Headless Chromium here runs at about 10 fps with SwiftShader; for stills use reduced motion, for motion `window.__tungsten` (dev only). In Git Bash, set `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1` before passing `#/work/...` anywhere or it gets turned into a Windows path.
- Use the helpers in src/lib/dom.ts (setVar, setText, setAttr, Spring) for per-frame DOM writes.
- The URL hash only ever holds an open show; in-page links scroll without touching it.
- Copy is deliberately minimal: names, networks, years. No bio. No dashes (en or em) in copy.
- Contact in `src/data/site.ts`: fullertonproductions@me.com and 416 882 1379 (shown spaced, no dashes; tel link +14168821379), confirmed by Miller 2026-10-15. Never invent or change contact details without him.
- Credits must be sourced. Accepted sources (Miller, 2026-10-22): NewscastStudio, TV Technology, James's own site (2016 in the Wayback Machine, photos in raw/archive-2016; and as it was in 2026, photos Miller downloaded in raw/site-2026), the family photo archive, and his IMDb credits (nm2333234). Don't add unverified credits, and don't guess years: leave a show undated rather than inventing one. Photos from his site that can't be placed in a show stay out until Miller says which show they are.
- `raw/` holds original photos and is gitignored; `npm run assets` builds new or changed images into `public/shows/` and `src/data/assets.json`.

Miller's ISP (Netcrawler) filters DNS through CIRA Canadian Shield, which flagged the preview hostnames as malware (2026-10-23); the production hostname resolves fine. If a preview won't load for him, that's why: NordVPN, Edge with secure DNS, or mobile data get round it.
